About Me . . .

Hi, I'm Eggy, and yep, I'm just another programmer in this world. I do backend and full-stack development, mostly living in Golang, Rust, and PHP (back in the day). I love dissecting low-level architectures, and naturally, I'm a big fan of the concept: No hidden control flow. No hidden memory allocations. No preprocessor, no macros.

When my brain needs an escape from queueing theory or microservices, you'll catch me playing games, watching anime or movies, and just zoning out to my go-to playlist.
